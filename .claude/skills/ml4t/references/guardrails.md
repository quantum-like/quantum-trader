# Guardrails: master checklist for ML4T research

Cross-cutting reference compiled from the guardrail sections of every chapter, case study and library reference in this skill. Every item is tagged with its source unit(s) so the detailed reference can be opened. Items that say the same thing in several chapters are merged once with all tags. Marks such as "(inference)" are carried over from the notes.

**Tag legend.** `chNN` = `chapters/NN_*.md` (e.g. `ch07` = `chapters/07_defining_the_learning_task.md`). Case studies: `cs_etfs` = `case_studies/etfs.md`, `cs_crypto` = `case_studies/crypto_perps_funding.md`, `cs_nasdaq` = `case_studies/nasdaq100_microstructure.md`, `cs_sp500eo` = `case_studies/sp500_equity_option_analytics.md`, `cs_firmchar` = `case_studies/us_firm_characteristics.md`, `cs_fx` = `case_studies/fx_pairs.md`, `cs_cme` = `case_studies/cme_futures.md`, `cs_sp500opt` = `case_studies/sp500_options.md`, `cs_useq` = `case_studies/us_equities_panel.md`. ## Pre-flight checklist

Answer every question before trusting or reporting a research result. A "no" on any line means the number is not yet evidence. Ordered by workflow stage.

**Data**
1. Have you profiled coverage per symbol and per period (entries/exits per year, all-missing periods, phantom sessions) instead of counting nulls inside rows? (ch02, ch07)
2. Are returns computed from total-return-adjusted prices, with the adjustment convention named per column, raw prices reserved for screens and fills, and `price_adjustment_factor == 1.0` on the last row? (ch02, lib_data, cs_useq, cs_cme)
3. Have you run corporate-action, ticker-reuse and `ret > 1.0 / prev_volume == 0` checks before any survivorship or portfolio statistic? (ch02, ch07)
4. Is every timestamp tz-aware (UTC-normalised), aggregated on the exchange session rather than the UTC/calendar day, and is the observation grid measured from timestamp spacing rather than read from config? (ch03, ch16, cs_fx, cs_nasdaq)
5. Are bar-open stamps advanced to bar close before any as-of join, and are timestamp units identical on both sides of every join? (ch02, ch03, cs_crypto)

**Point-in-time**
6. Are fundamentals, macro, filings, COT and 13F keyed on availability (announcement, acceptance, release, `available_from`) with vintages where revisions exist, never on period end or report date? (ch01, ch02, ch04, ch22, ch23, lib_data)
7. Is the universe point-in-time (eligibility from trailing data, effective the following period) and is residual list survivorship declared rather than claimed away? (ch08, ch14, cs_etfs, cs_useq)
8. Are entities keyed on permanent identifiers (CIK, CUSIP, `sec_id`, LEI), not tickers or free-text names? (ch04, ch22, ch23, cs_sp500eo)

**Labels**
9. Is the label anchored where the fill happens (next open / next-bar VWAP), not at the same close that generated the signal? (ch07, ch16, ch19, ch26, cs_nasdaq)
10. Have you reported `effective_sample_size` (N_eff ≈ N/h; a case-study helper in `case_studies/utils/label_diagnostics.py` and `07_defining_the_learning_task/06_ic_inference.py`, not an ml4t-engineer function) and overlap-aware standard errors for every overlapping label, with ml4t-engineer's uniqueness weights (`calculate_uniqueness=True`) / `sequential_bootstrap` where labels overlap? (ch07, ch11, cs_etfs, cs_cme, lib_engineer)
11. Were labels built on the complete sorted series before any eligibility filter, with every unlabelled row reconciled to one cause and the counts summing to frame height? (cs_etfs, cs_useq, cs_cme, cs_sp500opt)

**Features**
12. Is every cross-sectional statistic computed `.over("timestamp")`, every rolling statistic `.over(symbol)` and trailing-only, with no centred window, full-sample z-score, rank or winsor bound anywhere? (ch02, ch07, ch08, ch11, cs_useq)
13. Are flow, volume, volatility and premium predictors lagged one bar so no same-bar quantity enters a prediction? (ch03, ch08, ch21, cs_nasdaq)
14. Are model-based features (HMM, GARCH, Kalman, ARIMA, PCA, FFD) produced by a declared burn-in + refit schedule, filtered not smoothed, recursion inputs pinned to the training window, and did a holdout-withheld rebuild / truncation test pass with exact agreement? (ch09, cs_etfs, cs_fx, cs_sp500opt, cs_useq, lib_diag)
15. Are text and news signals lagged to the next session after acceptance/publication, deduplicated, and scored by a checkpoint whose training cutoff precedes the evaluation window? (ch10, ch13, ch22)

**Splits and protocol**
16. Does every split purge `label_horizon` sessions counted on the market calendar (not calendar days) and embargo at least the feature lookback wherever training can follow test? (ch06, ch11, ch12, lib_diag, cs_etfs)
17. Are splits made by date rather than row position, and is the holdout boundary set by label END date (`holdout_start − horizon` on each instrument's sessions)? (ch12, ch13, cs_fx, cs_sp500eo, cs_useq)
18. Were scalers, imputers, encoders, winsor bounds and percentile thresholds fit on training rows per fold only? (ch05, ch07, ch11, ch13, lib_engineer)
19. Is the holdout sealed: boundaries fixed at the start, opened once for confirmation, with no selection, ensemble rescue, threshold refit or second configuration after seeing it? (ch06, ch14, ch17, ch20, cs_fx, cs_nasdaq)

**Evaluation**
20. IC inference — (a) is IC computed per date then averaged, never pooled across stock-days; (b) is the t-stat HAC with lag ≥ horizon − 1 on the chronologically sorted series; (c) is a minimum cross-section enforced per date; (d) are NaN and null both dropped; (e) is `ic_n_days` coverage printed beside the mean? (ch07, ch08, ch11, ch13, cs_nasdaq, cs_useq)
21. Have you included a no-parameter baseline (zero or persistence forecast, majority class, random-signal plumbing backtest) and read coverage before any ranking? (ch10, ch11, ch13, cs_nasdaq, cs_firmchar)

**Multiple testing**
22. Was the searched set (features × horizons × configs × checkpoints × portfolio sizes) declared before results, logged, and corrected with BH-FDR on HAC p-values and DSR/RAS over all variants tried? (ch07, ch08, ch16, cs_sp500opt, lib_diag)
23. Is every checkpoint registered as its own candidate rather than the best epoch picked after seeing validation? (cs_fx, cs_sp500eo, cs_useq, cs_firmchar)

**Modeling and selection**
24. Is selection made on validation backtest Sharpe after costs (never IC), only among full-coverage candidates on common support, with exactly one field varied per sibling? (ch26, cs_fx, cs_nasdaq, cs_useq, cs_sp500opt)
25. Are conformal or other uncertainty outputs checked for empirical, regime-stratified coverage before any position is sized on them? (ch11, ch12, ch13, ch17)

**Backtest**
26. Backtest protocol — (a) `NEXT_BAR` execution (decide close t, fill open t+1); (b) every execution field set explicitly and confirmed to move something; (c) a benchmark run through the same engine, dates, fees and capital; (d) a random-signal plumbing test passed; (e) closed trades only for round-trip statistics? (ch16, cs_nasdaq, cs_firmchar, lib_backtest)
27. Is every Sharpe reported with sample length, PSR/MinTRL and DSR deflated by the full declared trial count? (ch07, ch16, cs_sp500opt, lib_diag)

**Portfolio and costs**
28. Are covariance conditioning, effective positions and solver status validated, and allocators compared against equal weight on common support under a cost sweep? (ch17, cs_sp500eo, cs_useq)
29. Are costs in commensurable units, turnover one-way, borrow/financing charged as time-held rates, impact coefficients labelled as assumptions, and the breakeven reported against the assumed cost rather than the top of the grid? (ch18, ch20, cs_etfs)

**Risk, live, MLOps**
30. Risk — (a) are VaR models exception-backtested (Kupiec at the stated level, clustering inspected); (b) are stops evaluated stop-first on the prior bar's water mark with entry-time ATR; (c) are sizing inputs (volatility, conviction) lagged one session; (d) are drift thresholds calibrated on a period with known drift and joined to realised performance? (ch19, ch26, lib_backtest)
31. Live — (a) one `Strategy` class for research and live; (b) field-by-field parity gates in CI with the gate count asserted; (c) a kill switch persisted across restarts; (d) startup reconciliation that refuses to launch until clean; (e) promotion gates fixed before results; (f) the broker mode asserted explicitly (`assert_paper_trading()` or `assert_live_trading()`) with host, port and account printed? (ch25, ch26, lib_live)
32. Are all numbers read from the registry or artifacts at run time (never typed into prose), populations declared before fitting, and partial or preview runs refused from canonical outputs? (ch20, ch26, cs_etfs, cs_useq)

**Synthetic, RL, LLM**
33. For synthetic data: temporal split, discriminative and stylized-fact checks beside moments, and `N_PATHS` populations rather than one path? (ch05)
34. For RL: reward paid as `p_{t-1} r_t`, features lagged, episode-level splits, paired SEs on shared seeds, and formation/evaluation separation? (ch21)
35. For RAG/agents: cutoff dates enforced after retrieval, market price withheld from prompts, citations validated against retrieved ids, tool policy enforced outside the prompt, and retrieval rankings judged with human labels? (ch22, ch24)

## 1. Data acquisition and storage

- **Vendor close ambiguity** — WIKI close is raw, Yahoo may be split-adjusted and varies by interface, the ETF panel close is fully adjusted / the same number means different things per provider, so returns and dollar floors silently change meaning / test on AAPL 2014-06-09 7:1; name the convention per number; Alpaca `adjustment="raw"` by default, Tiingo exposes raw and adjusted; detect discontinuities at split dates (ch02, lib_data).
- **Adjustment-basis mismatch when stitching providers** — Yahoo adjusts through today while WikiPrices stops 2018-03-27; stitched histories carry a false seam / a day's move baked into the rescale factor, double-counted seam rows, a stitch that silently needed no overlap / rescale on a shared date (volume by reciprocal), never measure the factor across the seam; `raise ValueError` on silent no-overlap; filter each leg on the seam date and print row counts; carry `source` + `processed_at` columns on multi-source panels (ch02, lib_data).
- **Half-formed current bar / in-progress session** — Yahoo returns volume with null OHLC for the forming bar (`DataValidationError: yahoo: Column 'open' contains 1 null values`); placeholder resolution is not calendar-predictable (AAPL still NaN 8.5 h after close) / intra-session data leaks into features and sizing / `_drop_priceless_rows`, `update_through_last_complete_bar`, retreat loop that stops when `end <= last_stored`; keep only sessions strictly before the current UTC date and reconcile against raw counts; `ProviderUpdater(safety_margin_minutes=5)` backs the end time off so an in-progress bar is not stored as final (inference) (ch02, ch25, lib_data).
- **Forward-filled gaps** — `DataManager.update(fill_gaps=True)` default writes hundreds of phantom weekend/holiday bars / invented observations / `fill_gaps=False` or the safe helper; `complete_sessions(fill_method="forward", zero_volume=True)` writes zero volume so synthetic rows stay identifiable (ch02, lib_data).
- **Gap detector without an exchange calendar** — holidays read as 1-day gaps, calendar-dense series report none, wrong cadence threshold per market / pair with a calendar-aware completeness check; `GapDetector(tolerance=0.1)` flags intervals > expected + 10% (inference) and suppresses overnight/weekend false positives; `CryptoCalendar.find_gaps(threshold_multiplier=1.5)` excludes maintenance windows; set the gap threshold in the break of the step distribution (5 days), universe-wide gaps are market events (ch02, lib_data).
- **Vendor revisions after first store** — Yahoo revises ~5 trading days, CRSP ~30 / stored history silently stale / `update(lookback_days=7)` overlap keeping newest; `FULL_REFRESH` instead of patching parquet in place; `--update` extends past a drifted `end:` config (ch02, lib_data).
- **Non-atomic writes and torn reads** — a crash mid-write corrupts the store / `atomic_write_parquet` (`.tmp` then `replace`); HiveStorage generations; stage → rename into `generations/` → fsync → commit manifest; single-writer `FileLock` per key (`lock_timeout=30.0`); `atomic_writes=False` rejected (ch02, lib_data).
- **Validator that only passes** — a check that cannot fail hides defects / inject faults; confirm the sort-monotonicity check fires on a deliberately shuffled frame; a leakage test built on a frame already filtered by the same condition only tests Polars (ch02, ch07, ch23, cs_cme).
- **Per-fetch schema and value integrity** — missing canonical columns, nulls, wrong dtypes, NaN/inf, negative volume, OHLC invariant violations (`ohlc_mode="strict"` raise / `"drop"` logs `n_dropped` / `"warn"`), duplicate `(timestamp, symbol)`, symbol identity mismatch all raise `DataValidationError`; FRED/Kalshi/Polymarket skip OHLC checks only / silent instrument drift otherwise / set `provider.ohlc_mode="drop"` for noisy sources; `StorageManager.load/update(enable_validation=True)` (lib_data).
- **Post-load screening presets** — `OHLCVValidator` (staleness 5 identical closes, `|return| > 0.5`), `CrossValidator` (gap > 10%, volume > 10x, weekend bars, outside 9:30–16:00 ET), `AnomalyManager` (MAD/z/IQR threshold 3.0, `min_samples=20`, `max_unchanged_days=5`); class presets equity 0.2/5/10.0/0.05, crypto 0.5/3/20.0/0.15, forex 0.1/10/5.0/0.02, commodity/futures `negative_price_policy="warn"` 0.15/5/8.0/0.05 / one threshold across MAD/Z/IQR is three cutoffs; extreme-return WARNINGs flag each series' largest real move / print cutoffs in return units; score flags against corporate-action columns; read `report.has_critical_issues()` (ch02, lib_data).
- **Phantom sessions and holiday-dated rows** — LFVN/END print on holidays; provider rows dated to holidays / fake trading days / keep sessions with ≥ 20% of the universe quoted; `MIN_SYMBOLS_PER_DATE=1_000` defines a market date (lower for subsampled panels) (ch02, ch23).
- **Price units and scaling** — ITCH price4 (/10000), DataBento nanodollars (/1e9), CHRIS cents vs dollars scale P&L 100x; futures `quote_scale = tick * contract_size / tick_value ∈ {1, 100}` / silently wrong magnitudes / heuristics median > 10000 or > 1e6; print min/median/max; `_normalize_price_units`, `ContractSpec.__post_init__`; read `tick_size`/`tick_value` from `data/futures/market/futures_specs.yaml` (ch03, lib_data, cs_cme).
- **Futures contract-data hygiene** — a hand-written `ASSET_CLASS_MAP` silently skips unmapped products; roll counts mixing tenors (deferred tenors flip often); bar length assumed from an argument name; different bar lengths joined on a bare timestamp; calendar spreads inside contract data (single-digit negatives, high volume); last trade ≠ expiry for thin listings; "5 days before Friday" counted in calendar days lands on Sunday; inner joins swallow uncovered days; episodes numbered on disagreeing rows only halve lengths; roll convention mattered least in zero-rate years / raise on unmapped products; count rolls on the front month only and report it separately; `describe_bars` first; collapse to a common grid before joining; `MIN_OUTRIGHT_PRICE` floor; third-Friday filter; session grid folding Sunday onto Monday; anti-join and report uncovered days; keep the no-rollback guard but do not credit it (count leader ≠ front days); the handover gap grows ~10x as financing − dividend yield widens, so measure it by year in bps (ch02).
- **Options chain data hygiene** — non-solved IVs are ~1/3 of rows; stale hand-written solver code tables (~10 codes, not 5); a spread model fitted to the median is p90 > 2x off in the wings; a constant risk-free rate across 2020 (the 1y fell > 1 pp); validating on `head(n)` (all 2 January); European formulas on American contracts; silently dropped solver failures; skew read off calls alone (the left half is ITM calls); thresholding Greeks bounds hides the informative theta line; 5-day overlapping windows (effective n ≈ 1/5); eight names in one year is not a cross-section; correlation summarises a non-monotonic quintile shape wrongly / filter `Converged`, never a time-value proxy; read code tables from the data; calibrate the spread model to the tail; per-date rate; seeded random sample and print days covered; express residuals as a `delta_error/gamma` spot gap and do not attribute them; count dropped failures; read skew from the put-call gap at the same strike/expiration; report share and count; non-overlapping subsample (ch02).
- **Timezone confusion** — ITCH is naive local never UTC; DataBento needs `replace_time_zone("UTC").convert_time_zone("America/New_York")` before RTH; a fixed UTC window is an hour wrong half the year; funding-hour checks in the wrong zone miss every window / naive → treat as UTC, aware → convert to UTC before comparing to 00/08/16 UTC; bound exchange dates in `America/New_York`; `assign_sessions(outside_session="raise")` to catch bad timestamps (ch02, ch03, ch25, lib_data).
- **Crypto funding and premium mechanics** — `F = P + clamp(I − P)`: the clamp is not a cap (realized rates run many times beyond ±(I ± c); the real cap is per contract) and is not applied to the premium (`clamp(P) + I` removes the dead zone); mean premium negative while mean funding positive; the premium close is not the funding rate (Binance uses a time-weighted average); exact zeros are not missing (1 in 7, up to 1/3 on thin contracts); an exact-timestamp join across frequencies matches 1 in 8; an as-of null count read as coverage (one symbol > 2 months stale); gap checks on the reference symbol only; premium units in decimals / score against realized rates and read funding direction from realized, never from the premium sign; do not filter zeros, choose tie handling; as-of join; staleness from the publication clock; run gap checks on every symbol; label "APY above threshold" and "clamp binds" separately; scale premium units explicitly (ch02).
- **Timestamp units and dtypes at joins** — Polars µs vs pandas ns; deep-learning prediction sets carry `us`, other families `ms`; a join on mismatched dtype returns empty with no error / `dt.cast_time_unit("us")` before joins; cast with `on_clock_dtype` at every join site; `dt.replace_time_zone("UTC")` before casting a naive column; never re-key the registry (`value_digest` is unit-sensitive) (ch03, cs_crypto).
- **Sequencing ties** — multiple messages per nanosecond; Polars does not preserve input order on tied keys / deletes applied before adds / sort `(timestamp, tracking_number)` / `(ts_event, order_id)`, deletes last explicitly (ch03).
- **Parameter caps and sample limits distort diagnostics** — `MESSAGE_LIMIT`/`MAX_MESSAGES` truncate each type independently; a capped `MAX_SYMBOLS` run ranks cross-sectional statistics within a different universe (a different quantity, not a smaller one); capped 04 vs 05 frames select different names / trust only complete days; run production with `MAX_SYMBOLS=0`; `apply_max_symbols` must reach every consumer; CI reads capped matrices for shape only (ch02, ch03, cs_nasdaq, cs_useq, cs_firmchar).
- **Derived data and output locations** — writing into `ML4T_DATA_PATH`; deployment artefacts mixed into the research registry; `Study.activate()` rewrites `ML4T_OUTPUT_DIR` process-wide / wrong registry read or written / write to chapter `output/`; open the study as the first statement before any `get_case_study_dir`, registry read or `BacktestExplorer`; `read_only_study` in reporting notebooks; glob sources from `REPO_ROOT` (ch03, ch25, cs_etfs, cs_nasdaq, cs_sp500eo, cs_firmchar).
- **Provider resilience and metered cost** — rate limits (Alpaca 200/min, FRED 100 of 120/min, Kalshi ~10/s, Polymarket 60/min CLOB + 30/min Gamma, Yahoo chunks of 50 with 1.0 s delay, Binance `max_concurrent=5`); circuit breaker opens after 5 consecutive `NetworkError`, half-open after 300 s; pagination cursors must progress; `validate_routes` refuses unroutable batches (`ProviderRoutingError`) / silent partial downloads and runaway spend / `estimate_cost()` / `--estimate-only` before any metered spend (`databento_acknowledge` requires `I UNDERSTAND`); `fail_on_partial`, `return_exceptions=True`; one provider instance per run (~30 ms httpx init) (ch02, lib_data).
- **Feed coverage** — Alpaca `feed="iex"` is ~2–3% of US volume (volume features biased low) / use `"sip"` (free plan lacks the last 15 min); unknown feed or bad `asset_class` raises at construction (lib_data).
- **Live queries in reproducible notebooks** — runs differ; CoinGecko doubles the last date; live search indexes move / `LIVE=False`, `RUN_LIVE_LLM=False`, replay from frozen logs; shipped snapshots; loaders raise `DataNotFoundError` naming the download command; `unique(subset="timestamp", keep="last")` (ch04, ch22, ch24).
- **Metadata and derived-column drift** — metadata falls behind data; `YIELD_CURVE_SLOPE = DGS10 - DGS2` duplicates `t10y2y`; derived columns drift from their formula / double weight, wrong values / `reindex` columns against metadata; recompute from inputs and compare to the metadata formula; run `duplicate_pairs`, `(df.diff() == 0).mean()`, PCA and Ward first-merge checks on any panel assembled by coverage filter alone (ch01, ch04).
- **Mixed native frequencies and trending levels** — quarterly series repeated monthly fake persistence; CPI/GDP levels make elapsed time the dominant variance direction (partition = calendar eras; extended model 4 episodes, 0 revisited) / check `native_frequency` and staleness share; enter trending series as YoY rates; check `episode_count` and `revisited_clusters`; silhouette rewards the failure (ch01).
- **Month-stamping error in resampling** — `group_by_dynamic(label="right")` stamps January as 1 February, so every downstream date is a month late / apply `offset_by("-1d")`; keep right labelling for honesty (ch01).
- **Blanket warning suppression** — `warnings.filterwarnings("ignore")` hides convergence, dropped-alt-text, sortedness and numerical warnings / scope by message, category and module (`FutureWarning, module="mlflow"`; the one LightGBM feature-name message); catch `ConvergenceWarning` into a grid column instead (ch02, ch09, ch12, ch13, ch17, ch26).
- **Benchmark hygiene** — memory-mapped reads, warm caches, truncated results posting the fastest time, acknowledge-early engines, timing the wrong operation (kdb+ `-8!` looked ~40x faster), aggregation semantics drift, size columns as compression ratios, client cost inside write bars (`iterrows` 10x `itertuples`), arithmetic mean of speedups, mismatched memory units (`estimated_size("mb")` is MiB), environment-dependent numbers / wrong engine chosen / state the cache policy; poll to queryable inside the timing; `uv run` and print versions; compare sizes only in one place; catch `Exception` not just `ImportError` for optional engines (ch02).
- **Logging noise** — `AQRFactorProvider` structlog output drowns load cells / `structlog.configure(wrapper_class=structlog.make_filtering_bound_logger(logging.WARNING))`; `MetadataTracker.check_health(stale_days=7)` for staleness (ch01, lib_data).
- **Credentials, paths and secrets** — plain-text secrets, credential URLs in errors, symlink escapes, `../x` keys / `validate_secrets`, `${ENV}` references only, owner-only YAML, `PathTraversalError`, `contained_path`; load env credentials only when `SUBMIT_PAPER_ORDERS=True` with an explicit `no_credentials` disposition; `EDGAR_IDENTITY` required (ch04, ch25, lib_data).
- **Synthetic fallback when real data are missing** — a demo that invents a corpus or panel publishes indistinguishable fake numbers under real headings / fail loudly: `load_documents` raises, 07 load failure is fatal, treat the log-normal fallback as demo-only (ch05, ch15, ch22).
- **Cache and artifact drift** — existence-keyed caches, edited parquet, regenerated corpora, stale DML or MLflow stores / old numbers under new source / signature-keyed caches (`assess_cache`, `_cache_is_usable`, `RETRAIN=True`); `.meta.json` / `.digest.json` sidecars with content hash, row count, keys, writer, input digests and estimation schedule; `EXPECTED_INPUT_SHA256`; assert `MARKET_DATA_DIGEST` across stages; `shutil.rmtree(MLFLOW_DIR)` before `set_tracking_uri` (ch11, ch15, ch22, ch23, ch26, cs_cme, cs_useq).
- **Papermill parameter injection** — constants derived inside the tagged cell run on defaults; trailing comments hide the name so a reduced run trains at production size / derive dependent constants in a later cell; no trailing comments on `MAX_TRAIN_STEPS`/`MAX_EVAL_SAMPLES`; keep request and resolved names distinct (`resolved = injected or declared`) (ch08, ch10, cs_sp500eo).
- **Figures assembled across cells** — Plotly/Jupyter capture half-built figures under Papermill / one figure per cell or helper; never set `MPLBACKEND=Agg` or `PLOTLY_RENDERER=json` in executed notebooks (ch02, ch12, ch26).
- **Legacy migration and storage config** — ambiguous key maps, typos in config / explicit `key_mapping`, re-read and `_verify_frame` compare, rollback on error; `StorageConfig` frozen with `extra="forbid"` (lib_data).

## 2. Point-in-time, survivorship and identifiers

- **Survivorship via backfilled snapshot or chosen universe** — WIKI symbols alive at collection backfilled to IPO (exits only from ~2014); Yahoo returns live tickers; hand-picked ETF/CME/crypto lists exist today; current-constituent rosters exclude every departed company / inflated results, wrong sign on survivorship studies / detect entries/exits per year and exit span < 80% of panel span; CRSP/Compustat with delisting returns for long spans; declare "describes these surviving funds"; never claim a survivorship-free estimate; PIT eligibility removes bias within the list, not of the list (`eligibility_note`) (ch02, ch08, ch11, ch14, ch15, ch16, ch17, ch18, ch19, cs_etfs, cs_fx, cs_crypto, cs_sp500eo, cs_firmchar).
- **Full-sample liquidity or volume screen** — later volume decides historical membership; admits exactly the stocks that stayed liquid / lookahead + survivorship / `turnover = traded_close * traded_volume`, keep (symbol, year) with `n_days >= 200` and `avg_turnover >= 10e6`, `eligible_year = year + 1`, write `eligibility.csv`; `close > 5` and `adv_21d > 1M` on a trailing 21-session window per decision date; fix `UNIVERSE_END` before any fold; dollar floors on traded prices never adjusted close; thresholds not inflation-adjusted is a known limitation (ch11, ch14, ch18, ch23, cs_etfs, cs_useq).
- **Period-end keying for fundamentals and filings** — a 10-K is filed 1–3 months and a 10-Q ~40 days after period end; text is not public until acceptance / uses numbers nobody had; the error equals the reporting lag / carry both dates; filter and ASOF-join on `announcement_date`/`filing_date` with frames sorted `[symbol, date]`, `by="symbol"`; measure `filing_date − period_end`; count rows with null knowledge time (ch04, ch08, ch10, ch22).
- **XBRL concept keying and filing-lag populations** — ASC 606 moved revenue to `RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax`, so a null concept is a tagging change, not a missing disclosure (imputing recovers nothing); keying on labels ("Net sales" vs "Revenue") breaks cross-company joins; filing lag has two populations (routine filings vs restatements) so the mean describes neither; original + amendment summed reads as extra coverage / measure coverage per concept and read the null pattern (industry-wide = tagging) before imputing; key on us-gaap concept names (Frames API) for cross-sectional work, labels only for single-company exploration; histogram the lag (20-day bins to 800 days) and read median and tail separately, treating late-attributed facts as knowledge-time-late; count a cell once if > 0 (ch04).
- **Ranking on knowledge time instead of valid time** — returns the most recently announced row; restatements walk the answer backwards / sort `[entity, valid_time, knowledge_time]`, take last per entity (ch04).
- **Filing date vs intraday acceptance** — after-close acceptance is tradeable next session; date-only matching leaves a residual one-session look-ahead (inference) / carry `acceptance_datetime`; shift to next session if acceptance > close or use next-day open (ch04, ch10).

## 3. Microstructure and bars

- **Order-book reconstruction errors** — replace chains lost (U issues a new reference absent from adds), deletes sized at original shares (double-count partial fills, negative levels), cold-start books hitting unknown pre-market references, C executions ignored, P/T prints applied to the book / corrupt depth and OFI / keep a live pool updated by U or pointer-double chains with a cycle cap; size deletes as `shares − removed_before` with deletes sorted last; process from start of day and print `unknown_order_count`; include C in removals; P (ITCH) and T (DataBento) are tape only, F updates the book; count `(spread < 0)` as the reconstruction error rate (ch03).

- **Trade-side classification** — ITCH P side is always 'B' (resting side) so signed bars degenerate to tick bars; DataBento side N unlabeled; tick rule misclassifies trades inside the spread; tick rule applied on bar close collapses OFI to the sign of the bar return / infer with tick test or Lee-Ready and assert both sides present; print the unclassified share and build every bar type from the same classified set; classify per trade inside `aggregate_to_bars`; prefer quote-based classification when quotes exist; apply ~1 ms (co-located) to ~10 ms (retail) lag for Lee-Ready in backtests; print coverage beside tick-test accuracy (ch03, ch08, ch18).

- **Session blending** — pre/post-market flattens the U-shape, inflates spreads and dominates flow regressions / filter RTH first (`regular_session_mask()` 09:30–16:00 ET); report null rates by session; never impute minute-bar trade fields (~20% null with extended hours); bound sessions by the published schedule and drop bars stamped after the scheduled close (vendor padding on early-close days feeds padded exit prices) (ch03, ch18, cs_nasdaq).

## 4. Labels

- **Anchor mismatch / same-bar fill** — label close-to-close but execution next open; scoring a prediction on the bar it was formed from credits a price the model could not trade; invisible in the mean, decisive per label / anchor where the fill happens (next open for EOD signals, next-bar VWAP intraday, `execution_delay: 1_bar`); pair score at close t with return t→t+1; where the release has no open price name the close-to-close label as a proxy and keep the gap in mind reading IC vs Sharpe (ch07, ch16, ch19, ch26, cs_nasdaq, cs_fx, cs_firmchar, cs_sp500eo, cs_etfs).
- **Close-only triple barrier** — touches detected only on closes and booked at the barrier price; gains understated, losses hidden (asymmetric, larger on the stop side) / pass `high_col`, `low_col`, `open_col`; library `_resolve_barrier_exit` uses the open for gap-through; `calendar_aware_labels` keeps windows inside sessions; to size the effect run a second labeling, never reprice the first (ch07, lib_engineer).
- **Barrier labels read as market description** — a −1% stop on a path that fell 28% discards the path / barriers only when the strategy really exits there; fixed-horizon returns to forecast magnitude; `reject_boolean_numeric_setting`, `validate_finite_numeric_setting`, `validate_max_horizon` (ch07, lib_engineer).
- **Class balance read as informativeness; cut-point drift** — barrier labels balance because the stop (1%) is tighter than the TP (2%); a constant class balance can hide a target whose economic meaning changes / compare against the majority-class baseline; `label_diagnostics` over time; plot the cut-point return series beside class proportions; measure the label domain on disk before choosing a metric (binary AUC on ternary `fwd_dir_8h_3c`) (ch07, ch12).
- **Overlapping labels treated as independent** — N_eff ≈ N/H (418,362 dev rows → N_eff 20,017 ≈ 1/21); naive SEs overstate by ~sqrt(overlap) (momentum naive t 5.06 vs HAC 1.44; naive +6.89 vs HAC +6.14 monthly); the loss is dominated by high-concurrency periods; on a 1-minute matrix each 15-min label is seen 15 times / uniqueness weights (`calculate_uniqueness=True`), `sequential_bootstrap`, `effective_sample_size(..., bar_col="session")` (case-study helper in `case_studies/utils/label_diagnostics.py`, not ml4t-engineer) on a grid built before any row was dropped (a label occupies h intervals, `ends = events + horizon − 1`); at the 1-day horizon the effective count must equal the row count; sample IC one observation per horizon; `labels.rebalance_step` 1/5/21 so a 21-day label is not traded daily (ch07, ch11, ch13, ch14, ch17, lib_engineer, cs_etfs, cs_cme, cs_fx, cs_useq, cs_nasdaq, cs_sp500opt).
- **Trend-scanning t as significance** — price-level regression residuals are autocorrelated; a driftless shuffled path rejects as often as SPY / use sign/horizon; calibrate any t against a demeaned-shuffle permutation null (ch07).
- **MFE/MAE windows and excursions as features** — a window including bar t counts pre-entry movement and stretches H to H+1; entry-bar high/low inflates excursions; excursions are forward-looking ("DO NOT use excursion values as ML features") / shifts −1..−H (`shift(-1)` before the reverse rolling max); measure from the bar after a close entry; use only to pick TP/SL (ch07, ch19, lib_diag).
- **Multi-tenor or multi-contract rows in forward windows** — `shift(-k)` steps across contracts, inflating excursions; `shift(21)` on straddle rows is 21 quotes not 21 sessions / filter to one tenor before shifting; keep `position == 0` before any shift; reindex onto the underlying session grid with `min_observations_fraction: 0.8` (ch07, cs_cme, cs_sp500opt).
- **Round-number barriers** — "TP = 2x ATR, SL = 1x ATR", "2% crypto": measured p75 stop for SPY/ES is > 3x ATR / ordinary movement hits the stop / calibrate from MFE/MAE quantiles per instrument and horizon using p75 of per-entry MAE(t)/ATR(t), not an aggregate ratio; recompute on each refresh; few closed calibration trades give noisy levels (priors seeding a search) (ch07, ch19).
- **Labels built after the eligibility screen** — the forward step counts surviving rows so the horizon spans removed rows / build `forward_return` on the FULL price history sorted (symbol, timestamp) before any filter; four asserts: incomplete tails null, window calendar span `<= ceil(7h/5) + 7` days, labelled rows == total − h·n_symbols, dtype Float64; reconcile every unlabelled row to one cause summing to frame height (missing exit quote, never-quoted premium, hole in hedge path, expiry past panel end) (cs_etfs, cs_useq, cs_cme, cs_sp500opt, cs_fx).
- **Holes and settlement clocks** — a hole inside the window pairs non-adjacent sessions; funding settles on a variable 2h/4h clock; a 60-row sequence can span > 60 settlements / `MAX_SESSION_GAP_DAYS = 5`, null where `_holes.shift(-h) − _holes != 0`, reconcile `labelled + tail + hole + no-anchor == height`; time-window rolling for funding sums; quote `eligible_rows` not panel height; `session_col` forward returns null the last `horizon` bars per session; time horizons use `join_asof` with `tolerance` (cs_cme, cs_crypto, lib_engineer).
- **Label built on the wrong instrument** — shifting a constant-maturity or "30-day ATM straddle" column returns a different contract most days; zeroing roll-day returns deletes real P&L / `instr_ret_*` is not a return anyone held / look up the held contract in the raw chain at entry and exit; held-contract return with roll cost in costs; inner join legs on (date, strike, expiration); `∏(1 − r) − 1` for a short (ch02, cs_sp500opt).
- **Null comparison yields a confident class** — `null > 0` is false, not null / unfillable rows receive a direction / explicit null guard when deriving `fwd_dir_*` (cs_sp500eo).
- **Label names ≠ task** — FinMarBa labels are the sign of subsequent price moves, not sentiment; market-neutral ≠ sentiment-neutral / inspect the source column (`Pct_Change`) before scoring (ch10).
- **Window label written back across covered sessions** — puts returns up to t+h−1 on session t / `label_at_window_close`; first label index ≥ `WINDOW_LEN − 1`; earn a regime label against the next session, not the same close (ch09).
- **Shifted trailing return as forward return** — coincides only when momentum window == horizon / price at both ends of the forward window (ch04).
- **Point-in-time percentile thresholds** — `rolling_percentile_*` uses only forward returns whose horizon has ended (availability index `t + horizon`); thresholds null until `min_samples` outcomes exist (`max(1, min(1008, lookback // 10))`) / changing a future price must never alter an earlier threshold (lib_engineer).
- **Heavy-tailed or capped targets** — squared error on a capped-above, unbounded-below target lets tails dominate / compare mse vs mae/huber and raw vs winsorized (`fwd_ret_1m_win`) at fixed features and folds as separately declared experiments; attach a signal to the label it holds on (cs_sp500opt, cs_firmchar).
- **Label count ≠ horizon count; label mixing** — two labels can share a horizon; the HTM option label with coherent costs and fixed-horizon straddle labels with bps costs are not comparable / map labels to trading days (`HORIZON_DAYS`), fail on unknown labels; `LABEL_RESTRICTIONS` pins the comparison label (ch15, ch20).
- **Sizing on uncalibrated probabilities** — AUC-optimised classifiers rank but are not calibrated / calibrate before `compute_bet_size`; reliability diagram; hit rate by confidence quintile must rise monotonically (ch07, ch11).

## 5. Features

### 5a. Direct (price, volume, fundamental, macro) features

- **Unsorted data before rolling ops; re-sort after joins** — windows silently misalign; stringified dates sort lexicographically; `join_asof` with `by=` cannot verify sortedness / sort once at load `df.sort(["symbol", "timestamp"])`, re-sort after every join feeding a rolling op, assert grid spacing; `compute_features` sorts by `[*group_cols, timestamp]` (set `assume_sorted=True` only when order is truly temporal); explicit sort + assert before suppressing the sortedness warning (ch07, ch08, ch10, cs_etfs, lib_engineer).
- **Full-sample z-score, rank, winsor or scaler** — future cross-sectional moments; panel dispersion more than doubles between quietest and loudest year; composite liquidity scores carry post-bar information / `.over("timestamp")` for every cross-sectional statistic, never `pl.col(x).zscore()` on the whole column; per-date quantile clipping (`winsorize_features`, `clip_within_date` at 1st/99th); expanding-window percentiles or rolling z-scores; cross-sectional thresholds (month's own median) may be whole-sample, any time-axis threshold or fitted scaler must be re-estimated inside each training window; trailing-window scores with closes through t only (ch07, ch08, ch11, ch16, cs_useq, cs_sp500eo, cs_firmchar).
- **Rolling windows crossing symbol seams** — an unguarded shift pairs one firm's return with another's / `.over("symbol")` on every shift/rolling expression; the library applies `.over(group_cols)` to every expression and refuses `group_col=[]` when an asset column exists (ch18, lib_engineer).
- **Contemporaneous flow features** — same-bar OFI correlation is mostly the definition of an up bar; TRF prints are delayed / `shift(1)`; compare same-bar vs lagged correlation and treat the gap as the size of the look-ahead; lag `order_flow`, `price_impact`, `hidden_liquidity` by one bar (ch03, ch08, cs_nasdaq).
- **Shared endpoint between feature and label** — a feature ending at p_t and a label starting at p_t share the same price, so noise in p_t enters both with the same sign: spurious covariance by construction / shift the label one session; treat failing cells as re-measure, not disproof (ch07).
- **Volatility estimator mismatch** — Parkinson/GK estimate within-session vol (read low vs close-to-close); ATR is a dollar range; VWAP minute prices understate RV; a 4-for-1 split reads as −75% / use close-to-close or Yang-Zhang for daily sizing, Parkinson/GK for intraday range; ATR for stops/normalisation only; last-trade prices, raw-bar open/close, XNYS calendar, flag |log return| > 0.25 (ch08, ch09).
- **Thresholds on relative volume** — a ratio cut selects different days than a log-volume z-score and does not transfer / write rules on the log-volume z-score; measure overlap; name transforms precisely (a rolling median is not a percentile rank) (ch08).
- **Lead-lag from stale closes** — a less liquid ETF closing before SPY fakes a lead / verify the leader traded at the lead timestamp (ch08).
- **Missing deferred contracts and carry** — carry becomes NaN; `x12` carry is a scale factor not an annual rate; panel heights mislead sign reading / require paired tenors, handle rolls explicitly, `carry_rank_sector`; count signs (ch08, cs_cme).
- **Options surface policy changes mid-backtest** — delta convention, interpolation, maturity mapping; IV read within a maturity window moves with expiration changes / contaminates every options feature / version construction choices with the features; keep the policy fixed; acknowledge DTE-bucket limitation (ch08, cs_sp500eo, cs_sp500opt).

### 5b. Model-based features

- **Fitted on the whole sample or inside a CV fold** — GMM/HMM/GARCH/ARIMA/Kalman/centroids/PCA/factor loadings fitted on all history assign values informed by later dates ("not a rule that could have been applied in 1929"); fold-wise fitting gives training rows parameters from their own future and validation rows not, with nothing raising ("a schedule bounds an estimate; a fold selects rows") / `walk_forward_feature(X, timestamps=, burnin=, refit_every=, fit=, apply=, n_features=, freeze_after=, on_fit_error="skip")` with no `fold` column; burn-in 504 GARCH / 756 regime, refit every 63 (21 for options), `LOOKBACK=1260` / `REFIT_EVERY=63` for breaks; stamp from the last completed refit; parameters from strictly earlier sessions; fit factors, loadings and the IPCA mapping inside each training window (ch01, ch09, ch14, cs_etfs, cs_fx, cs_sp500opt, cs_useq, cs_crypto, cs_cme).

- **Regime-conditional and interaction columns** — `feat_bear/neutral/bull` sit on unsigned −1/0/1; non-zero interaction values are not regime counts; mixture of experts with thin regimes; near-zero regime importance misread / assert the pairing; report session counts per regime; require 20 training rows per expert; `volatility` already carries the information (ch09).

## 6. Splits, cross-validation and evaluation protocol

- **Label leakage at the fold boundary** — forward labels of horizon h near the boundary embed validation prices; a 21-day label resolves inside the next window; an unpurged boundary also contaminates conformal calibration residuals / inflated validation scores, silently / purge `label_horizon` sessions (`label_horizon` in `WalkForwardCV` / `CombinatorialCV`, `label_buffer="21D"`); assert `va[0] − tr[−1] − 1 == h` and `min(purge_gaps) >= horizon`; embargo cutoff at `−(LABEL_HORIZON + 1)`; intraday purge H+1 (16/6/61/16 min) because entry at next-bar VWAP and exit H later consumes t+H+1; library default `label_horizon=0` purges nothing, always pass the true horizon (ch06, ch07, ch09, ch11, ch12, ch13, ch15, ch19, lib_diag, cs_etfs, cs_nasdaq).
- **Missing embargo** — backward-looking features of post-validation training rows consume validation prices in CPCV/k-fold / `embargo_size >= feature lookback` (`embargo_pct=0.01`, `EMBARGO_PERIODS = 3`); zero only in pure forward walk-forward; `embargo_size`/`embargo_td` and `embargo_pct` are mutually exclusive; splitter aliases resolve to canonical keys (`label_buffer -> label_horizon`, `feature_buffer -> embargo_td`, `val_size -> test_size`, `holdout_start/end -> test_start/end`, `calendar -> calendar_id`) (ch06, ch15, lib_diag).
- **Shuffled or random splits on time series** — trains on the future to predict the past; `CalibratedClassifierCV` and domain-classifier CV default to non-chronological folds; random TSTR splits leak / chronological walk-forward only (`TRAIN_FRACTION = 0.7` earliest); `shuffle=True` with `dates` raises in `train_test_split`; expanding chronological calibration splits with a 21-session purge; contiguous relative-time blocks (`DOMAIN_CV_TIME_BLOCKS=20`) (ch05, ch06, ch11, ch19, lib_engineer).
- **Split by date, never by row position** — a row-offset cut puts one decision date on both sides and cuts cross-sections; CV folds, `block_permute` and temporal halves counted in rows become a handful of entities on one day / compute boundaries on unique dates and assign each example by date; mask on timestamp groups; the cut moves to the last timestamp-change index so equal timestamps stay together; pass `groups`/`units` to `block_permute` (ch12, ch13, ch15, lib_engineer).
- **Input windows across a boundary are NOT leakage** — the past is available at decision time / purge only on the label side; partition DL examples by target date (ch13).
- **Holdout contamination** — any development decision made with the holdout destroys the unbiased estimate; sweeps clamp `END_DATE` from `evaluation.holdout_start` in `setup.yaml`; the holdout is spent once at the end against the whole candidate set / set `holdout_start`/`holdout_end` at the start; open once for confirmation; compute labels from already-filtered prices; summaries (mean, spread, median, null-count) only over the development period, shape counts may span the whole; `refuse_a_second_look` and `_refuse_a_selection_disagreement`; software cannot prevent "run 500 times and quote the best" — conduct rule: ask once (ch06, ch08, ch11, ch13, ch14, ch17, ch20, cs_fx, cs_sp500opt, cs_sp500eo).
- **Holdout boundary by label END date** — a row observed before `holdout_start` whose outcome resolves inside it is a holdout row; a trade opened before and expiring inside is a holdout trade / usable boundary = `holdout_start − horizon` on each instrument's own sessions (`fold_boundary_date`, NYSE calendar; `holdout_endpoint_cutoff` within session intraday; settlement date for options with `labels.buffer: 35D` = a full option cycle); seal diagnostics on `_label_end = timestamp.shift(-h).over(symbol) < HOLDOUT_START`, `LAST_SCORABLE_DECISION_DATE = pre_holdout_sessions[-(h+1)]`; training cut at `LABEL_AVAILABLE_AS_OF = days_before_live[-H-1]` for live (ch25, cs_fx, cs_sp500eo, cs_useq, cs_sp500opt, cs_cme, cs_etfs, cs_nasdaq).
- **Refit window uses the widest label buffer** — the same training window serves whichever label selection landed on; a 1D buffer leaves ~three weeks of `fwd_ret_21d` outcomes reachable (inference on count) / end training 21 observations (not calendar days) before the first holdout session; crypto: `holdout_start − 24H`, refused to default to zero; read the label from the selected lineage and step back along its observation grid (cs_fx, cs_crypto).
- **Holdout scored with the validation-fitted model** — circular ("already happened once here"; a registry actually contained such a set) / refit with training ending `labels.buffer` before the window; assert `holdout training hash != validation training hash`, raise "did not refit"; `registered_holdout_generations` flags "VALIDATION-FITTED − not out of sample"; retire via lifecycle (`holdout_generations_to_retire`), never row deletion (cs_sp500opt, cs_useq, cs_firmchar, cs_nasdaq, cs_cme, cs_etfs).

- **Pooled IC instead of per-date IC** — pooling mixes which days were good with within-day ranking, weights dates by firm count, is dominated by day-level co-movement, and a persistent tilt masquerades as accuracy / cross-sectional IC per date (or session, or month) then average; quantile boundaries within date; net out the session mean; pool only when each period is rank-normalised (ch04, ch07, ch08, ch10, ch14, ch26, cs_firmchar, cs_useq).

- **Headline IC without SE, sign or coverage** — the same IC can be clearly positive or indistinguishable from zero; an inverted ladder means shorting the wrong leg; a model predicting a constant on some dates posts high IC on a self-selected sample ("a metric averaged over a set the model itself selected is not a metric") / report IC, ICIR, HAC t, CI, dispersion; read sign first and at every horizon; read `ic_n_days` / `full_coverage` before `ic_mean`; `ic_mean_daily` from `prediction_metrics`, not the catalog fold mean; assert scored dates > 0 (ch07, ch08, ch12, cs_etfs, cs_fx, cs_sp500eo, cs_useq, cs_nasdaq).

- **Fold summaries as uncertainty** — std of per-fold IC is not an estimator; folds are few, overlapping and disagree in sign (the most adverse fold moves more than the rest combined) / cite `ic_ci_lo/hi`, `ic_t_hac` from the daily series; per-fold IC as a stability box plot; Driscoll-Kraay 95% CI forest plots; a family whose CI crosses zero is "not established", not "fifth" (ch12, ch13, ch23, cs_etfs).

- **Comparing ICs scored on different days or windows** — a shorter or easier window can win alone; retired generations that scored every day clear the coverage bar (3 of 9 case studies topped by a retired set when omitted); weekly series in a daily pool compare dates not models / `select_rank1` keeps max `ic_n_days`; joins keep `gbm_days == other_days`; mismatches to an "Excluded / Masked for unequal coverage" table, never silently plotted; `exclude_prediction_hashes=_retired`; `common_sample_daily_ic` on intersected keys; one representative (config, checkpoint) per family on full decision-day coverage; "complete" is measured against the case study's own fold grid; `covers_fold_grid`, `MAX_FOLDS` (ch08, ch12, ch13, cs_etfs, cs_nasdaq, cs_useq, cs_sp500eo).
- **No-parameter baseline** — two models can both be worse than predicting zero and still rank; majority rate is well above 0.5 in a long expansion; a model can beat AUC 0.5 and lose to the accuracy baseline; persistence is hard on prices, weak on returns / always include zero forecast (returns) or persistence (prices), report MSE / zero-MSE with go/no-go < 1; per-fold majority baseline; re-argue the baseline per series (ch10, ch11, ch13).
- **Reading R² out of sample** — intercept shift and noise-amplifying coefficients drive R² < 0 even with correct ranking; rank IC on a price level scores near one / use IC and read R² against zero; cross-sectional IC for multi-asset work (ch11, ch13).
- **IC rising with horizon / half-life markers** — overlap autocorrelates IC and understates SE; a rising profile reads as backwards decay / be skeptical of monotone-in-horizon IC; mark a half-life only after the peak; check `by_label` whether IC rises with horizon; expect thousandths intraday vs hundredths daily (ch07, cs_nasdaq).
- **Significance as tradeability** — a factor can pass the HAC test and lose after costs; a single raw indicator (21-day momentum IC ~0 on ETFs) is not a signal / break-even and cost models (ch16–18); liquidity-bucket IC; feature engineering and model combination (ch07).

- **Short track records and lumpy samples** — small IC plus autocorrelation needs large T (1–2 years rarely enough); 2003–2025 holds one severe recession, one pandemic, one inflation episode; two folds with 2020 as one; the ETF long-short t-stat is not significant in the full sample or either era / `min_track_record_ic`; minimum detectable IC from the HAC SE; era stress tests with the failure mode named in advance; caveat every fold-level claim ("enough to say an effect is not large, not enough to characterise one") (ch01, ch06, ch07, cs_sp500opt, cs_sp500eo).

- **Write the IC series first, read it back** — every figure/average must come from the stored copy (`ic_timeseries.parquet`) (cs_fx).
- **Group leakage in supervised discovery** — nodes from the same DAG share a dataset / `StratifiedGroupKFold` with group = dataset id; no cross-fold preprocessing state (ch15).
- **Single chronological split as a ranking** — one split, one seed, no intervals; IC differences within sampling noise / treat as demonstrations; rank only with walk-forward across case studies; multiple 252-session windows for any selection claim (ch13, ch23).

## 7. Multiple testing and selection

- **Selection bias / max of N trials** — the max of N null ICs ≈ sqrt(2 ln N) σ_IC; the best null of 30 shows a respectable Sharpe and high PSR; validation artifact = max over a sweep so every validation→holdout arrow "decays" by construction ("the figure manufactured the decay it was meant to measure"); rank-1 of 3,663 backtests is partly chance / never report the selected statistic uncorrected; BH/Holm/RAS or DSR; log the search-set size; compare like with like (holdout IC vs holdout Sharpe; validation vs holdout Sharpe of the SAME selected configuration); show the distribution each max came from; "winner / champion / verdict" language refused, "holdout decay reading" only (ch05, ch07, ch16, ch17, ch20, ch27, cs_nasdaq, cs_sp500opt).
- **Declared search set** — the set must be fixed by the rules that generated the features before any result is seen; adding candidates after seeing results, or counting only what looked promising, understates K; grid count = product of axes (7x5x4 = 140); abandoned variants are forgotten / trial taxonomy + automatic logging; BH-FDR at `FDR_ALPHA = 0.05` over the whole declared set (40 features, 5 in 04; ~63 columns; ~50 candidates: plain threshold passes 13 of 49, overlap-corrected 3, FDR none); count configs x checkpoints x portfolio sizes as the trial count; K includes all label variants (~2,300 backtests from `fwd_ret_1m_win` and `fwd_class_1m`) but excludes superseded generations; record the arm in the ledger `note` (ch08, ch16, cs_sp500opt, cs_sp500eo, cs_fx, cs_crypto, cs_nasdaq, cs_firmchar, cs_useq).
- **Naive p-values into corrections / wrong `label_horizon`** — dependence passes straight through; omitting `label_horizon` infers bandwidth from T and emits an anti-conservative warning / `compute_ic_hac_stats` p-values with the real horizon: `NON_OVERLAPPING` (1) for independent periods, 5/21/63 for daily-sampled multi-day returns (ch07).
- **FDR and HAC are different corrections** — one corrects for feature count, the other for one feature's persistence; on a 1-session label NW costs almost nothing while FDR still bites / apply both and report separately; print the three counts (naive |t| > 1.96, HAC |t| > 1.96, FDR rejected) and `survivor_share` (a naive t of ~10 is worth ~3 after overlap correction) (cs_useq, cs_etfs).
- **Correlated candidates under BH** — a feature and its within-session rank give identical IC yet count twice; overlapping return windows make the effective number of tests smaller so BH is conservative; `FDRSettings` warns because BH assumes independence/PRDS / cluster near-duplicates on 1 − |corr| at `REDUNDANCY_CUT = 0.70` (Spearman over every ~200th date; 22 clusters) and name one representative; count evidence not columns (4 premium features = 1 ordering); use the stability route to PROCEED (effect-size floor + fold sign consistency), never read PROCEED as a stricter BH; compare PROCEED only within a case study (floors differ 0.003–0.01), BH-FDR across (ch20, lib_diag, cs_useq, cs_firmchar, cs_crypto, cs_etfs).
- **Exploration vs confirmation** — exploration-pass discovery rates inflate; an exploration-route promotion "has not been confirmed by anything" / 80/20 exploration (alpha 0.10) then confirmation on held-out data (alpha 0.05); promote on fold stability; triage STOP if coverage < 0.70 or staleness > 0.50; PROCEED if `fdr_significant` OR (`sign_consistency >= 0.60` against the feature's OWN pooled sign AND |mean IC| >= 0.01); REVISE for `date_level_feature`, `insufficient_data`, `not_significant_standalone`; the screen is evidence, not a filter (model notebooks train on the whole matrix); nothing downstream filters on the triage ledger except `20_strategy_synthesis/02_feature_evaluation.py` (ch07, cs_etfs, cs_fx, cs_nasdaq, cs_sp500eo).
- **Stability cut on share of positive IC** — contradicts |IC| selection; a steady inverse predictor scores zero / cut on sign consistency (max of positive share, negative share) at 80% (60% in the ETF ledger); count folds agreeing with the feature's own sign; derive the reported sign from counts (ch08, cs_useq, cs_nasdaq).
- **Rademacher anti-serum (RAS) scales** — a standardized R_hat deducted from an IC rejects everything; kappa from the averaged IC invalidates Hoeffding; data-dependent kappa has no coverage guarantee; annualizing or demeaning before RAS mixes units (hourly series under-charged ~6x) / Massart ratio on standardized ICs, deduction in IC units, print both; `KAPPA = 1.0` for inference (empirical max as sensitivity); fix the bound before seeing data; per-period volatility-standardized (ddof=1), non-demeaned returns, annualize last; print `data_snooping_penalty` and `estimation_error`; library RAS `delta=0.05`, `kappa=0.02`; more observations shrink estimation error, not `2*R_hat` (ch07, ch16, lib_diag).
- **Effective number of trials** — 100 variants are not 100 trials (over-penalises); raw count with correlated candidates over-deflates; never drop a correlated set to independence; trial variance measured across 100 trials swings by a third between draws / Rademacher `complexity_ratio`; `effective_number_of_trials` / `K_eff` (floored at `min_k_eff`, `effective_trials` and `correlation_method` mutually exclusive); feed ALL variants to `compute_selection_adjustment`; `DSRSettings.check_n_trials` warns on suspiciously low counts; disclose variance uncertainty (ch07, ch16, lib_diag).
- **DSR mechanics and misreads** — `sharpe_ratio`, `expected_max_sharpe`, `deflated_sharpe` are per period while `sharpe_ratio_annualized` is annualized (a sqrt(252) factor between rows); DSR is a tail probability, RAS a lower bound; non-normality raises DSR when SR_hat < benchmark and lowers it above / annualize all rows before tabulating; threshold = `benchmark_sharpe + sqrt(variance_trials) * [(1−γ) Φ⁻¹(1−1/K) + γ Φ⁻¹(1−1/(K e))]`, γ = 0.5772156649; PSR variance `1 − skew*SR + (kurt−1)/4 * SR²`; PSR for a single strategy, DSR for a selected one; tearsheets compute undeflated DSR unless `n_trials` is passed; DSR deflated over the full declared grid size K (under-deflation from a smaller stage is the failure the check catches); "read a deflated Sharpe as the price of having searched" (ch07, ch16, lib_diag, cs_sp500opt, cs_etfs).
- **PBO misread or faked** — a no-edge selection sits AT 0.5 and sampling error on 50 combos is wide; independent IS/OOS matrices do not implement PBO; a decayed edge is flagged unstable with non-exchangeable blocks / want PBO near 0 and the IS-best near the top OOS; each row = one complementary split of the same series; CSCV needs ≥ 2 folds and ≥ 2 variants, `C(n, n/2)` combinations; PBO is a model-selection diagnostic kept separate from DSR; two-fold PBO is too coarse either way (ch07, ch16, lib_diag, cs_sp500eo).
- **MinTRL and track length** — low Sharpes need > 10 years unsearched and `never` after a handful of candidates; SE ~1 on one year of daily data / `compute_min_trl` / `min_trl_fwer` with the search's `variance_trials`; report PSR/CI; note the `_mintrl_color` ratio `observed_sr / min_trl` looks dimensionally inconsistent (inference: intended `current_periods / min_trl`) (ch07, ch16, lib_diag).
- **Grid readings and post-selection p** — 30 cells yield 1–2 false positives by chance; picking feature + horizon by looking at the scan must be paid for; sign flips across three regimes are three tests with chosen split points / BH across the whole grid, star corrected cells; carry the grid-wide decision beside the raw p; require IC range ≥ 0.04 before building interactions and add them to the searched count; one knob at a time in sweeps (ch07, ch08).
- **Mechanism checks: colliders, confounders and low-power flips** — conditioning on a common effect (fund flows) manufactures association; bivariate checks miss multivariate confounders (the most dangerous confounders are other features); small wrong-sign ICs in subsamples are noise; shifted rolling features share (L − Δ)/L inputs so placebo persistence is mechanical / draw the DAG first and never condition on variables caused by both feature and label; Ch15 partial IC, DML and sensitivity analysis; STOP only if the opposite-sign |t| > 2 AND the unconditional IC is not significant; treat placebo persistence as a diagnostic, not a gate, and weight lags beyond the lookback (252d for 12-1) (ch07).
- **Best lookback from a sweep** — the maximum of N correlated estimates is biased upward / apply RAS; require a broad near-optimal region (≥ 90% of peak), not a point; read the ridge-path shape with HAC bands, neighbouring alphas are usually indistinguishable (ch08, ch11).
- **Event-window search** — extending the CAAR window until the band excludes zero; pre-event drift in signal-triggered studies; event clustering; overlapping estimation and event windows; ~100 breakouts on 5 ETFs / fix the window in advance; report the post-event window separately; clustered SEs or one event per day (BMP/Corrado); `min_gap_days=21` (30 in text); integer offsets on one shared date index; events lacking full windows rejected with `EventRejectionReason` (ch08, lib_diag).
- **Harvey bar on published factors** — survivors were selected on t = 2 / apply t ≥ 3 to the Newey-West mean-return t-stat, not to Sharpe; scale the bar to candidates tried (ch17).
- **Checkpoints and epochs as hidden trials** — keeping a config's best iteration reports the max of 10/8/20 numbers as one; `epoch_at_high` is post-hoc; "epoch where validation Sharpe was highest" wins a validation-Sharpe contest by construction; eight near-identical checkpoints of one network make an allocator comparison a measurement of one prediction / register every checkpoint as its own candidate; ensemble members enter at `checkpoint: last`; schedule from physical epochs; `checkpoint_dominates`, `epoch_against_capacity`; cap `checkpoints_per_config`; count distinct configurations with `top_n_cap` (cs_fx, cs_sp500eo, cs_useq, cs_firmchar, cs_nasdaq, cs_etfs, cs_sp500opt).
- **Never select on IC** — IC ignores costs, turnover, tails and spread; IC and Sharpe orderings routinely differ; `load_prediction_index` ends `ORDER BY m.ic_mean DESC NULLS LAST` so any positive `top_n_predictions.signal` screens on rank correlation / `top_n_predictions.signal: 0`; select only on validation backtest Sharpe after costs; rank by `ic_mean_daily` but select on `backtest_metrics`; keep the two tables separate (ch26, cs_fx, cs_nasdaq, cs_sp500opt, cs_useq, cs_firmchar, cs_sp500eo).
- **One field per sibling** — changing allocator / stop / cost AND anything else backtests a different strategy with nothing looking wrong; cadence confounded with case study; a 4-way CAE vs SAE difference / leaf-by-leaf spec diff vs the baseline sibling (model, checkpoint, signal, allocation, execution, costs, rebalance rule, account config, price-frame identity); vary universe and cadence one at a time; match bottleneck, hidden units and lr before attributing (ch20, cs_fx, cs_nasdaq, cs_sp500eo, cs_sp500opt).
- **Cost rows and previews never enter a selection cohort** — the ranking would report the kindest cost assumption; preview rows resolve a reduced catalog / cost variants are descendants excluded from 15–19; cost sweeps run after every selecting stage; previews freeze nothing, select by label, never enter populations; `CandidateSet.create` refuses preview members (cs_fx, cs_sp500opt, cs_crypto, cs_useq).
- **Seal the cohort before ranking; immutable populations** — a cohort that can gain members after selection, a family added or dropped after results, or registry row order deciding ties makes the choice non-reproducible / declare named populations in one notebook before fitting (`OfficialPopulation.create` before execution, `require_complete()`); seal {signal, allocation}; reproduce the selection from the immutable set or fail; `SUPERSEDES_*` for intentional republish; snapshot expected identities ("a right-looking number computed over a set nobody can reconstruct") (cs_fx, cs_sp500opt, cs_useq, cs_cme).
- **Rank on common support with deterministic tie-break** — a candidate scored over fewer days ranks beside full-coverage ones; exact Sharpe ties happen by construction (two allocator rows reproducing equal weight); `ORDER BY` alone is silently wrong; set iteration order and string hashing produced different ranks / `rank_backtests_on_common_support`; tie-break on backtest identity; sort ties by feature name, `rankdata(method="average")`; drop tied rank-one cells (`HORIZON_EXCLUSIONS`) rather than break ties; sort by `(timestamp, signal, symbol)` with ordinal ranks (ch06, ch12, ch16, cs_fx, cs_nasdaq).
- **Cross-label selection** — picking the best across labels selects the label as well as the model / each label keeps its own lineage; primary leader from primary candidates only; group by `(label, family)` everywhere (ch26, cs_sp500eo).
- **Holdout CI spanning zero is not failure or decay** — the window cannot distinguish the strategy from no edge; twelve monthly observations / print the interval beside the point; classify "Statistically unresolved"; computed sentences report only intervals wholly above or below zero; gate `gate2_holdout_diff_not_excludes_zero_negatively` stated so it can fail; holdout Sharpe and holdout IC need not agree (ranking inverted on holdout is exactly what the holdout exists to report) (ch20, cs_fx, cs_firmchar, cs_etfs, cs_nasdaq).
- **Allocator and baseline selection after the fact** — trying allocators until one works is multiple testing; testing against whichever baseline looks best makes the interval a selection artefact; ex-post ensemble rescue invalidates the holdout / identical inputs and protocol, count degrees of freedom, select before the holdout opens; fix the reference in advance and report gaps to all baselines; fix the ensemble before holdout scoring (ch17, ch20, ch21).
- **Hand-picked stress windows and publication-year splits** — chosen after the fact / label as description, never as a test of insurance or decay (ch17, ch19).
- **Unequal experiment budgets across families** — the maximum of more draws is higher even when nothing improved; 28 vs 150 configurations give incomparable precision / display `family_counts` / `n_configs` beside `best_validation`; keep `ic_best` and `ic_mean` on separate axes; read the per-family ECDF; discount wide-grid families (ch20, ch26, cs_firmchar).
- **Multiple testing in causal discovery** — 4–7 assets x lags x pairs yields dozens–hundreds of tests; `pc_alpha` is PC1 regularisation, not an MCI correction; max F over Granger lag orders / `fdr_method="fdr_bh"` in `run_pcmci` or `multipletests(method="fdr_bh")` over all source-target-lag p-values; `pc_alpha=None`; p per lag order BH-corrected or one joint test at fixed lag 5 (ch15).
- **Screens on a dozen alternatives** — the largest of 12 exceeds the largest of 1 under the null / report the count; fix the heatmap scale at ±2; call it screening; a 15-pair text screen likewise (ch04, ch10).
- **Model-notebook ICs carry no inference** — averaged rank correlations with no HAC adjustment in 05–08 / exposition only; per-feature claims from the HAC notebook, family claims from `ic_se_hac`; read family leaders by `covers_zero` first (cs_firmchar, cs_etfs).

## 8. Modeling and tuning

- **Singular or collinear design** — `skip_recent_*`, `mom_accel_*` are exact differences of raw returns; unregularised OLS on 46 correlated features splits coefficients arbitrarily and chases relationships that reverse / drop composites before OLS; VIF > 10 flags the rest; regularise or use tree ensembles; heavy penalties, read the IC-vs-alpha curve crossing zero mid-grid; `Ridge(alpha=1.0, solver="svd")` for Gram condition ~8e6 (ch04, ch11, ch13, cs_fx).
- **Row-order statistics on a panel** — DW/BG/Newey-West on a date-ordered panel measure cross-sectional dependence; `cov_type="HAC"` runs across the cross-section (21 rows = a fifth of a day); gap-crossing pairs filed as "lag 1" / regroup residuals per symbol, count lags on the session grid, collapse to one series per date; Driscoll-Kraay `cov_type="hac-groupsum"` with `time` (lag 21); age weights by session (ch11, ch15).
- **Robust SEs as a cure-all** — they fix variance only; misspecification leaves coefficients inconsistent / inference tools; judge by OOS IC (ch11).

- **Same-bar look-ahead** — a close-derived signal filled at that close earns a return that began before the signal existed; `SAME_BAR` and `vectorbt` preset curves are "what same-bar filling is worth" / shift the whole weight matrix or entry/exit conditions one row; `ExecutionMode.NEXT_BAR` (decide close t, fill open t+1); a bar's total volume is known only at close (pass accumulated or lagged volume); trade one bar after the scored bar (`next_bar_open_or_vwap`) (ch16, ch18, cs_nasdaq, cs_sp500eo).
