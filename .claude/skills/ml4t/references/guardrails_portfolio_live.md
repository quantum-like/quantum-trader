## 10. Portfolio construction

- **In-sample frontier and selection on the reported strategy** — every frontier point is where the optimizer would have put you given the data it saw; ranking candidates by validation Sharpe then reporting the winner overstates; full-sample and train/test tables do not share weights / estimate through `TRAIN_END`, freeze, score later; never pick an allocator or parameter from the test result; keep a holdout untouched; label validation-ranked allocations as diagnostics (ch17).
- **Ill-conditioned or singular covariance** — inversion divides by near-zero eigenvalues from redundant assets (11 assets = 55 covariances vs 30 = 435); walk-forward inversion fails silently; a better condition number is not better realized risk / compute κ(Σ) and the spectrum first; Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage; narrow the universe; `MIN_HISTORY=60`; validate weights with a visible equal-weight fallback and per-method counter; `rebalance_target` returns None to keep the prior target; compare risk models point-in-time (ch14, ch17, ch19).
- **Expected-return noise and corner solutions** — endpoint CAGR rests on two prices per asset; min variance / min CDaR load into the lowest-risk asset as hard as max Sharpe into the highest-return one; HRP's inverse-variance splits concentrate (a majority in one bond fund on 15 ETFs); risk contributions and drawdown targets do not transfer to the test covariance / prefer allocators that drop μ (min var, risk parity, HRP, EW) or shrink; effective positions, largest weight, HHI; trace bisection splits; add constraints; recompute risk contributions on the test covariance; HRP's case is N near or above T (5 assets over 252 observations is well-conditioned) (ch17).
- **Wrong risk-contribution formula** — `w_i d(var)/dw_i / var` carries a factor two and sums to two / `w_i (Σw)_i / (wᵀΣw)`, assert shares sum to one, verify with the diagonal oracle (IV gives 1/N) (ch17).
- **Solver output silently invalid** — SLSQP/CVXPY return infeasible, NaN or budget-violating weights; max Sharpe with no positive expected excess return is ill-posed; `max_weight < 1/N` cannot sum to one / `validate_optimization_result(... tolerance=1e-6)`, `validate_weights`, `assert_optimal_status`; `max_sharpe_regime` precheck → all cash (`cash_precheck`); `project_capped_weights` raises; pre-check Riskfolio/CVXPY versions (ch17, ch23).
- **Library units and containers** — PyPortfolioOpt wants annualized moments, Riskfolio/skfolio daily; weight containers differ; `FamaFrenchProvider` divides by 100, `AQRFactorProvider` does not / estimate once in daily units, convert at boundaries, `align_weights`, verify matched objectives agree to `COMMON_OBJECTIVE_TOLERANCE=1e-7` and weights to `WEIGHT_TOLERANCE=1e-5`; keep the percent-vs-decimal sanity check; drive heatmap axes from one symbol order (ch17).
- **Allocator comparisons without inference** — iid Sharpe SE read as a comparison (allocators share assets and dates; SE ~ 1/sqrt(years)); one split, one universe, one window; an allocator that wins at 5 bps may lose at 20 / paired block bootstrap of the Sharpe difference; `IR * sqrt(T)` as a t-stat; report orderings as descriptions of one period; cost grid [0, 5, 10, 20, 50] and compare only via the zero-cost row (if the ranking reorders between 0 and 20 bps no advantage is established); `COST_SCENARIOS = [5, 10, 15, 20]` (ch15, ch17).
- **Turnover misreads** — frozen weights with daily rebalance understate turnover; turnover bundles re-selection and sizing; equal weight is not a floor; gross Sharpe gains bought with turnover are unproven until priced; regularisation does not lower turnover; a monthly top-N re-pick trades most of the book / walk-forward re-estimation; separate entering/leaving from retained names; `compute_turnover()`; report gross and net Sharpe and turnover; compare to equal weight (gross ≈ net) (ch11, ch17).
- **Sharpe from vol rather than signal** — long-only top-N inherits universe beta; lower realized vol raises Sharpe without return; a zero risk-free rate flatters ratios over 2022–23 and can reorder strategies / read return, vol, drawdown and IC separately; `RISK_FREE_RATE` set to a cash rate (IR unaffected) (ch11, ch17).
- **Risk and performance statistics misread** — empirical VaR/CVaR 1-in-100 on a few dozen points never extrapolate past the worst day; library `up_down_capture` inverts the down reading; stability R² absorbs a multi-month drawdown; max drawdown has no interval / quote both, not as forecasts; ratio of mean monthly returns compounded to month ends; read beside the drawdown table; never rank overlays on small drawdown differences (ch17, ch20).
- **Kelly** — `f* = Σ⁻¹μ` has no budget, funding rate, cap or liquidation rule; fractional Kelly only scales it; second-order Kelly breaks under leverage; rolling fractions swing across an unactionable range; Sharpe hides ruin on a path that went to ~0 / explicit leverage constraint; empirical growth optimizer with positivity constraint; treat Kelly as direction and cap; `min_wealth_multiple`; order tables by gross exposure (ch17).
- **Correlation vs beta, factor histories** — correlation is scaled by both vols; dollar-neutral is not beta-neutral; long factor histories are gross of financing, turnover, capacity and tax; a factor loading is not alpha / exposure regression on a common window; cost it in Ch16/Ch18; read each beta against the 500-portfolio placebo interval (`run_placebo_benchmark`; FF5+MOM R² structurally limited on bonds/commodities/currencies) (ch17, cs_usfirm, cs_etfs).
- **Overlapping forward-return labels in allocation** — inflate Sharpe, break drawdown accounting, manufacture IC precision / global every-h-th schedule with no fold-boundary reset; annualize by 252/h; HAC with ≥ h−1 lags (ch17).
- **Deep allocator training discipline** — label leakage across partitions and via the last date (no realized return); stochastic layers or shuffled loaders with the two-pass loss; train Sharpe rising after validation flattens; unfair ablation (RNG moved by building it after training) and single-seed inference; leverage convention mismatch; NaN objectives and sparse history; SoftMin regime gap read as the objective's score / assert endpoint sets disjoint, `val.indices[0]==train_end`, decisions end at `dates[-2]`; `DROPOUT=0.0`, `shuffle=False, drop_last=False`, `prepend_predecessor`; checkpoint at the validation peak; snapshot `initial_state`, change only `softmin_lambda`, repeat seeds; normalize all allocators to `sum abs(w) = 1` per date through one net-return function; skip NaN evaluations, 80% coverage gate, metrics N/A below 10 observations; library: causal `TemporalAttentionBlock`, `CrossSectionalAttention(lag=1)`, `burn_in`, `gamma_cost=0.5`, `turnover_penalty`, `max_grad_norm=1.0`, `softmin_sharpe(tau)`, early stopping (`eval_every=10`, `metric_ema_alpha=0.45`, `patience=20`), `normalize_cross_sectional_weights` / `apply_turnover_limit` with `raw_weights` beside `processed_weights` (ch17, lib_models).
- **Signal collapse and vol-driven churn** — a signal piled at zero is "declined to trade"; rolling-vol jumps masquerade as indecision / histogram of the signal, `signal_at_bounds`; plot signal and vol separately; price persistent position change (ch17).
- **Equal weight is the baseline, never an allocator row** — EW rows do not exist at `stage='allocation'` (old filters returned all-null `ew_sharpe`); EW re-entering the menu; the EW `backtest_hash` collides with its baseline parent (48 rows lost) / read EW from the signal stage (`is_unallocated`); `15_portfolio_management` raises if it reappears; equal-weight baseline first, allocators second; a shortlist on EW Sharpe cannot find an allocator that rescues a buried model (design bound) (ch20, cs_sp500eo, cs_usequities, cs_cme).
- **Allocators misapplied** — covariance allocators (risk_parity, mvo_ledoit_wolf, hrp) at 15-min cadence; allocators sized on underlying equity returns for straddles; moment-based allocators with observations < names held (12 monthly bars identify only top_k=5); a shared lookback starves some allocators; inverse-vol called risk parity / skip covariance allocators at short cadence (allocators set sizes, not turnover); known limitation for options; one declared window per allocator (63 default, 126 for `mvo_ledoit_wolf`), not swept; name heuristics by construction (ch19, cs_nasdaq, cs_sp500opt, cs_usfirm, cs_usequities).
- **Two correlated models as two signals** — the portfolio holds one signal at twice the weight; strongly correlated family leaders are one view twice / pairwise prediction correlation on shared dates with panel keys intact; watch joins that multiply rows (cs_usequities, cs_usfirm).
- **Breadth on decision dates** — a sample average hides thin stretches; universe counts cycle with the expiration calendar (>2/3 absent on narrow dates; 77–469 names) / count on decision dates against `BREADTH_FLOOR = max(top_k_grid) = 20` (100 for the equities panel), `2 x top_k`; `max(session_date)` per truncated week for futures (cs_etfs, cs_cme, cs_usfirm, cs_sp500eo, cs_usequities, cs_sp500opt).
- **Long-short book collapsing** — fewer than `2*TOP_K` ranked names makes legs overlap and spreads ~0; duplicate names overwrite weights; every forecast negative in a risk-off cross-section makes top-K a basket expected to lose / raise on `< 2*TOP_K`, duplicates in a leg, a name in both legs; filter predicted return > 0 before the top-K cut (ch25, ch26).
- **Gross exposure normalization under regime scaling** — without gross normalization a market-wide scale only changes turnover costs / gross-normalize long-short weights within each date (ch15).
- **Effective names vs equal weight; centrality rules** — EW is the maximum N (1/HHI), only the shortfall is informative; the centrality-vs-weight plot is the rule `w ∝ 1/(c + 0.01)` itself / compare to inverse volatility; `CENTRALITY_FLOOR=0.01` never removed (ch23).

## 11. Transaction costs and capacity

- **Non-commensurable cost units** — per-share + per-contract + bps + spread added without error; `FuturesSlippage` returns dollars not per-unit; a contract count mislabeled as shares; fee schedules read on one date / store rates as text, classify each `costs:` block, fill `configured_bps` only when bps are stated; normalize futures with the multiplier; record `SOURCE_CHECK_DATE`, status and source per row (ch18).
- **Volume x price with mismatched series** — adjusted close x share volume or tick-count volume is not dollars traded; FX tick volume is an activity indicator only / check what each column measures; `supports_usd_capacity` / `volume_contract`; dollar floors on traded prices; `coverage_start`/`coverage_end` in every summary (ch02, ch18, cs_etfs, cs_fx).
- **Turnover accounting** — one-way turnover x round-trip cost already charges both legs; turnover against stale weights misstates trades; trade-count ceilings ignore notional; borrow folded into a per-trade cost accrues with time held on the short side / one-way = 0.5 Σ|dw|; compare target with pre-trade drifted weights; daily round-trip NAV turnover; borrow as an annual rate on short notional (~50 bps/yr flat is a known limitation; `net_sharpe_floor: 0.3`) (ch18, cs_usequities).
- **Spread estimators** — Corwin-Schultz and Roll can rank correctly yet be off by an order of magnitude; CS clamps to zero on most ETF and S&P 500 sessions; Roll at daily frequency on liquid large caps is overwhelmed by positive autocorrelation; CS widens with volatility on its own; mega-cap spreads as an anchor; tier-assigned spreads are not measured / validate on quoted data, report Pearson r AND identity-line R²/MAE/bias; report zero-share beside the level; check the lag-1 covariance sign; decompose rate vs level; median per-symbol volume-weighted spread; sanity-check tiers by turnover (median falls ~5x per tier step) and re-price under harsher spreads (ch18, cs_etfs).
- **Integrity ledgers and seams** — discarded rows nobody counts turn the benchmark into a different benchmark; windows counted in rows (20 rows = a month daily, a week on 8h bars) / integrity ledger summing back to the raw count; trade and quote panels separate; aggregate to a common daily grid first (ch18).
- **Look-ahead in cost and execution inputs** — liquid symbols by full-sample volume; trailing windows ending on the day described; bar volume in `calculate(volume=...)`; observing the current interval's regime; state bins or VWAP profiles from all data; fitting and evaluating the VWAP profile on the same sessions / descriptive only or select from information available at the time; shift by one; condition on interval t−1 with a training-only prior; quantile bins and profiles from training sessions; split at a fixed calendar date (ch18).
- **Impact model assumptions** — η or library defaults treated as measured; same-minute flow/return slope read as impact (news causes both); OLS on minute returns set by jump minutes; log-regressions dropping negative slopes; participation against daily ADV when volume concentrates at open/close; swapping linear for sqrt without rescaling; comparing model levels without matching coefficients (sqrt includes σ); near-straight concave curves misread as convex; `PowerLawImpact.min_impact` taken for a fixed fee; extrapolating sqrt impact; capacity assuming pro-rata spread; flat `PERSISTENCE_FRACTION = 0.5`; sign errors on sells; coefficients in raw price units / keep `eta_assumption` separate from measured σ and calibrate from TCA; label associations; Huber and accept negative R²; report excluded counts; `adv_factor` or intraday curves; match at a reference participation (0.01); `CombinedCommission(fixed=...)` / `PerShareCommission(minimum=...)`; stop capacity curves at `MAX_FEASIBLE_PARTICIPATION = 0.20`; state capital, turnover, participation limit and concentration; assert sell fills < decision price; quote temporary impact as bps at 100% interval participation, permanent as bps at 100% ADV (ch18).
- **Execution simulation optimism** — `ExecutionResult.adjusted_price` / `impact_cost` are placeholders (zeros); capped orders silently filled; executing through thin bars; aggressive caps; small test orders never exercising the cap; one session; tight VWAP tracking read as cheaper; predictable schedules; impact charged against volume including the order's own shares; force-filling at the close; partial-session grids / take price from the `Fill` the `FillExecutor` emits; carry `remaining_quantity`; `min_volume` floor and report blocked bars; four-panel participation diagnostic; size the parent at 0.5 of measured ADV; run all held-out sessions, bias separately from std/MAE/worst; pick the benchmark that matches the decision; randomize or adapt; treat results as lower bounds; cap in the last interval with a 100 bps shortfall penalty on the residual; keep only sessions with full `N_BUCKETS` (ch18).
- **Almgren-Chriss and TCA** — continuous-time κ approximation misstates the optimum; λ has no natural scale; comparing schedules on independent draws; linear temporary impact front-loads too little; closing-price benchmark on one execution; TCA splits of shortfall into impact and timing need a counterfactual price / exact discrete sinh solution; label schedules by liquidation half-life; shared shocks with antithetic pairs (N even); price under the matched power law; average over many executions; name the model behind any split (ch18).
- **Cost stack, cliff and cadence** — in-sample cadence rankings (fixed universe, no holdout); cost scenarios too close to discriminate (1 bp); fixing the wrong layer (a leveraged long-short loses more to financing/borrow than trading); passive/worked fills assumed; optimistic intraday slippage hides the cost cliff; the cliff approximation excludes financing, taxes, fill risk, impact uncertainty; `SpreadSlippage` default treats the input as a full spread charging half per side (passing a half-spread doubles the error); commission-sensitivity scalars do not transfer / descriptive only; make stacks differ materially; decompose attribution; validate against an actual execution process; check annual cost / gross return; check `convention`; re-run on the target universe; the cadence table is description and turnover multipliers are stated assumptions (ch18, ch20).
- **Breakeven interpretation** — break-even read against commission alone bounds total round-trip cost with the path fixed; linear breakeven and turnover x fee ignore forgone compounding; a ceiling above the swept grid is not a breakeven; gross and top-of-grid Sharpe decide nothing; a high breakeven clears a floor only; the slope matters, not the height (height carries every prior selection); the band is not selection-adjusted / compare against commission + spread + expected impact from the re-simulated sweep; `_breakeven` returns `observed=False` and censored rows are marked; interpolate at the case study's assumed cost (`setup.yaml`), report bracketing points and the breakeven/assumed ratio; the declared level must sit inside the grid; respect the lower band's zero crossing; at 15 min the cost decides the sign (ch16, ch20, cs_nasdaq, cs_usequities, cs_usfirm, cs_sp500eo).
- **Weights fixed across a cost sweep; cost curve as turnover measurement** — a real implementation trades less as fees rise; a single-rate Sharpe hides turnover; a constant rate cannot show that turnover rises when spreads widen; a single cost assumption flatters the more active allocator / disclose: steeper than well-managed, shallower than naive; read the breakeven vs the quoted band as a validation-period bound; sweep and run the micro-cap-realistic grid (ch16, cs_fx, cs_usfirm).
- **Cost normalization per instrument** — one cost line over raw returns hides an order-of-magnitude range (0.43 bps NQ to 5.80 bps ZC); a move in an ineligible fund was never available / divide each |move| by its own product's spread or `cost_bps.shift(h)` (`cost_bps = 2 * (half_spread + per_share) / median_traded_close * 1e4`) so break-even = 1 everywhere; measure only over eligible fund-years; a uniform sweep over a per-asset spread map is sensitivity, not pricing; per-share cost on split-adjusted prices is exploratory (`PRICE_CUTS = [50, 100, 250]`) (cs_cme, cs_etfs, cs_sp500eo).
- **Options cost model** — equity bps-of-notional understates option spread cost by 1–2 orders of magnitude and ranks the universe by volatility; charging two spread crossings on an HTM position; margin opportunity cost (15–20% of notional at ~5%/yr) omitted; mid-to-mid label pricing; spread fractions mistaken for achieved fills; contracts whose chain ends early pay exit spread + commission / `estimate_pct_of_premium`, sweep `cost_fractions`; HTM charges entry-leg half-spread only (fixed-horizon labels need entry + exit half-spread + 2x commission); `costs.components.margin_opportunity_cost`, `cost_dominance_note`; executable backtest against quotes where spread dominates (single-name options, intraday equities); the three-label decomposition (ch20, cs_sp500opt).
- **Cost siblings and grids in the registry** — `_non_cost_projection` must equal the chosen result's; expect exactly `len(labels) * len(cost_grid)` rows (44); all-in grid value split 50/50 with the backtest charging the total; fail on any missing grid point; refuse a null cost axis; the uniform grid cannot reproduce the maker/alt split; funding is not on the cost axis / read cells by `cost_runs` hashes; `set_backtest_costs_bps(spec, commission_bps=level/2, slippage_bps=level/2)`; `PER_SHARE_COMMISSION` from setup.yaml with no fallback; report `funding_pnl` beside execution cost; flat cost across 30 products is linear in turnover and a lower bound on scale costs (do not read breakeven as capacity); fixed fee tier per symbol is declared, not searched; price one strategy via `resolve_solvent_carrier` with its overlay carried, never per label or pre-overlay (cs_crypto, cs_cme, cs_etfs).
- **Capacity, financing and free data** — no capacity constraint (positions sized regardless of volume; `micro_cap_concentration: 0.5` floor); swap/financing unpriced in FX (`components` is a taxonomy); free data costs integration and maintenance hours / compute cost-recovery and target bps at the fund's AUM; report as limitation; Ch18 perturbs only the aggregate (ch04, cs_usequities, cs_fx).
- **Candidate promotion gross vs net** — a candidate that trades more shows better gross and worse net / charge each model for its own turnover at the midpoint cost (ch26).
- **Fixed-bps costs in RL** — bps per trade is the spread; impact depends on participation / `SquareRootImpact` with ex-ante σ and ADV; test across coefficients [0, 0.1, 0.3, 0.6] and liquidity cohorts (ch21).

## 12. Risk management

- **VaR validation** — publishing VaR without exception counting (it is a frequency claim); Kupiec only counts (clustered exceptions pass); historical VaR cannot exceed the worst loss in window; Cornish-Fisher with large kurtosis can worsen coverage; sqrt-time scaling needs independence / `backtest_var` + Kupiec at the stated level, exception ratio near 1; inspect clustering (add an independence test, inference); compare with Student-t/CVaR and include a crisis; never prefer Cornish-Fisher automatically; estimate the horizon directly (ch19).
- **Regime-label leakage in risk** — same-day return or full-sample thresholds define the regime; average-only diversification (correlations rise in crises) / prior-close vol, expanding thresholds, minimum history (252 days in 01, 60 in 06); split by lagged volatility state (ch19).
- **Volatility forecast loss** — ranking by MSE treats under-forecasting (the dangerous side) symmetrically / QLIKE; the squared-return proxy is noisy (ch19).
- **Exit-rule evaluation** — signal-driven entries contaminate exit tests; same-bar ambiguity (target-first manufactures winners); ML exits with positional mapping, no purge, no delay; overlay tightening (a hybrid inherits the tightest rule); cross-section incomparability; current-ATR vs entry-ATR; in-sample thresholds / single future window; separation ≠ profit; MAE/MFE as stop rules / random seeded entries with SE of each mean; stop-first, gaps fill at next open, state the order as policy; chronological split, purge `LABEL_HORIZON` rows, shift one session, key by timestamp; read exit-type composition; compare within a section; one `VolatilityStop` instance remembering entry ATR; calibrate to 2021-12-31 with a 30-bar embargo and a future test; re-run trades with the rule and costs under a frozen objective; diagnostics only (ch16, ch19).
- **No costs in exit or sizing simulations** — barrier-price fills, no slippage/spread/partials; a 3-tranche scale-out pays 3x fixed costs (enough to reverse a comparison); rules differ mainly in exit frequency; tight stops at short cadence fire on ordinary fluctuation and each exit is an unrequested round trip; overlays add trades charged later / price with Ch18 before any claim (02, 03, 08, 10, 11 all flag this); read the gradient across thresholds, not one row; sweep tightest to loosest with the unprotected line beside it, read the time exit first (ch19, cs_nasdaq, cs_usequities).
- **Sizing inputs** — day-t weight from day-t volatility (known after close); fractional shares overstate risk-budget precision; conviction scores untied to outcomes / window ends the session before; integer shares and recompute stop risk; bounds 0.2x–2.0x and validate the score first (ch19).
- **Stop implementation details** — wrong rule order records a double-breach bar as a win; reusing a stateful `ScaledExit` remembers fired targets; a trailing stop set from the current bar's high uses information the order did not have; controls act on bar closes only (intra-bar breaches fire later than live); stops are path-dependent on bar/fill config / stop first; one instance per position; high-water mark through the prior completed bar (`TrailStopTiming.LAGGED`); do not compare stop results across bar/fill configurations; know which price fields the fill model reads (ch19, lib_backtest, cs_fx, cs_usequities).
- **Attribution** — contemporaneous or full-sample betas; plain OLS SEs; delta-method omission; library-default mismatch; residual screens (|corr| > 0.15 has no null); Mahalanobis spikes mixing tails and estimation error; attribution ambiguity under correlated factors; factor sample bias (2010–2024 is one expansion plus one shock) / `compute_return_attribution(lag=1)`, rolling betas labelled next session; HAC lags 5 everywhere, block bootstrap when decision-relevant; pass every shared setting explicitly; find an economic driver and test OOS; monitor condition number, Ledoit-Wolf before inversion; declare a basis; state coverage (ch19, lib_diagnostic).
- **Trade-level diagnostics** — SHAP joined on the exit date or positionally explains another decision; worst trades chosen by realized loss mix failures with unlucky correct calls; trading any forecast > 0 fills the list with untaken trades (620 round trips) / stable trade ID, SHAP from entry-decision features (`TradeShapExplainer(tolerance_seconds=0.0, missing_value_strategy="skip")` returns `TradeExplainFailure` rather than approximating); check the forecast gap, clusters are hypotheses; 5 bps buffer or real costs (ch19, lib_diagnostic).
- **Stress tests and Monte Carlo** — single-crisis / single-asset shocks preserve diversification a crisis removes; hand-dated windows; free daily rebalancing in crises; Gaussian draws understate every tail; the 99.9% quantile on 10,000 paths rests on ~10 paths; drawdown on concatenated regime days invents a path / several windows across growth and rate shocks with simultaneous shock vectors; validate observed-session endpoints and test boundary sensitivity (inference); apply Ch18 costs as an implementability upper bound; kurtosis-matched Student-t with variance-preserving scale and list omitted mechanisms; report deep quantiles as order of magnitude; one-day conditional metrics (ch19).
- **Round-number and sample-relative thresholds** — VIX > 25, trend ±5%/10%, 1.3x median, tercile splits are not fitted; 10% / 2% / 5 / 100 ms breaker limits are plausible not calibrated / state as diagnostics; what a desk can absorb depends on capital and investors (ch19, ch26).
- **Drift thresholds and retraining loops** — uncalibrated PSI (0.10/0.25) and AUC (0.60/0.70) thresholds alert constantly or never; retraining on every alert shortens the window and guarantees the next alert; covariate drift read as concept drift (PSI/Wasserstein/domain AUC never see P(Y|X)); cadence slower than the market / calibrate on a period with known drift, set threshold and window together; require performance degradation plus ≥ 2 critical or ≥ 3 warning in the last 5 checks; join drift to realized performance ("no drift + performance down" means check concept drift); match cadence to regime speed; library consensus: PSI < 0.1 green, 0.1–0.2 monitor, ≥ 0.2 investigate, AUC ~0.5 none / 0.6 weak / 0.7–0.8 moderate / > 0.9 strong, flag only above `consensus_threshold=0.5` (ch19, lib_diagnostic).
- **Drift test mechanics** — PSI on tiny samples or different bin counts; closed outer bins drop the strongest drift evidence; PSI quantile bins on a point mass; random CV in the domain classifier memorises periods; permutation p-values on autocorrelated rows; hand-picked crisis episodes and a crisis inside the reference / fix quantile bins on the reference (`n_bins=10`) with the outer bins open to ±inf and read per-bin contributions; `DOMAIN_CV_TIME_BLOCKS=20` with all symbols at one timestamp in one fold (the all-row fit only ranks features); descriptive threshold (half the reference std); estimate the false-alarm rate on unselected rolling windows (ch02, ch19, lib_diagnostic).
- **Stacked exit models and clause logic** — an exit model trained on in-sample entry probabilities learns an accuracy production never supplies; a 24h label on a fold's last row reads into the next; quantiles refit on test; AUC as the strategy metric; a dominant OR clause collapses to whichever fires every bar; third-decimal AUC gaps on one interval / purged expanding OOF; fold-local thresholds; train-only 0.95 quantile applied unchanged; report both metrics, per-clause fire rates, more windows (ch19).
- **Learned hedging / CVaR objectives** — quoting the OCE objective as CVaR (it is an expectation over learned w); omitting liquidation cost rewards carrying inventory; epoch selection on validation; benchmarking a learned policy on its own training generator (GBM lacks jumps and clustering); unconstrained outputs; flat costs / empirical CVaR on validation/test; charge open, rebalances and liquidation; freeze 100 epochs, touch test once; validate on strictly later market data; hard exposure, stop-loss and daily-loss limits outside the network; log every input and position; 10 bp flat is optimistic (ch19).
- **Limits as governance** — net-only exposure limits let market-neutral books lever gross; a limit class enforces a number but not escalation; limits checked on a snapshot never exercise post-breach behaviour; no portfolio-level controls restrain the aggregate book / limit gross and net; write escalation, approval and reinstatement procedures; test halt-and-resume with `PortfolioState` rebuilt every bar; a drawdown cap or daily loss limit is a separate declared control (ch19, cs_fx).
- **Calibration and evaluation windows for rules** — calibrating on the evaluation window is a fit; grids plus three selection methods are an unadjusted comparison; calendar schedules drift from trading bars; missing assets become silent exceptions / calibrate 2019–2021, evaluate once on 2022–2023; read as one realized outcome; explicit decision timestamps; fail closed before the backtest; MAE-calibrated thresholds excluded in v3.1, only predeclared thresholds compete (ch19, cs_sp500eo).
- **Stale artifacts and opaque inputs** — outputs nobody reads go stale; loading an unavailable conformal artifact cannot support a result / write only for a named consumer; use the versioned Ch17 implementation with its data contract (ch19).
- **Non-point-in-time macro in risk reports** — a finalized FRED snapshot is not vintage / no macro conclusions; PIT vintages for backtests (ch19).

## 13. Live trading and MLOps
